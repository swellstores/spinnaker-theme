Spinnaker is a free theme for stores that want to be seen: big type, bright blocks of color, no shyness.

It is bold, warm and playful, and it fits any kind of store: headlines as big as the screen allows in a wide, heavy display face, sections on flat blocks of vermilion, sunshine yellow, deep ink and soft blush, and cards with thick outlines and solid offset shadows. It works as well for a handful of products as for a few hundred.

**An opening that stands out.** The hero puts your headline on one block of color and your photo on another, side by side on wide screens and stacked on phones, or lets the headline run the full width on its own. With no photo yet, it shows a pattern of circles and stripes drawn in your theme's colors, so a new store looks finished from day one.

**Categories as color tiles.** Your categories appear as big tiles that take turns through the theme's colors, each with its name set large, its own photo in a circle (or filling the tile) and a round arrow.

**Products on bands of color.** Show products as a grid or a sideways rail on a band of any theme color, under a heading as large as you like. Cards show a second photo on hover, a "Save 20%" or "Sold out" sticker from your own prices and stock, color dots, the price on a pill and quick add. On category, search and all-products pages the same band adds the product count, sort, folded filters and page links.

**A product page on two blocks.** The photos sit on a tile of color and stay in view while the buy box scrolls beside them on its own block, with the product's name set very large. Options, stock, subscriptions, pairings, the description and attributes come from Swell; add your own promises, notes and text under the buy button.

**Your brand's voice, big.** A statement block sets two lines of your words across the page in two colors, with an optional round sticker. A color split pairs a photo with a block of words and a button, and feature blocks give up to four short points their own colors and big numbers.

**Your look, without code.** Every block takes one of the theme's colors, so changing a color in Wave's Theme tab recolors every block that uses it. Set fonts, type size, spacing, page width and buttons there too, or ask the agent to recolor the whole store.

**Every page, built in.** Like every Wave theme, Spinnaker lays out every Swell page type: home, category, product, search, cart, account, blog, content pages, policies and more. The cart opens in a drawer, and customers can track orders, buy again and manage subscriptions. The header and footer are shared by every page, and every fixed word shoppers see is yours to reword in Store text.

**Edit it in Wave.** Click any section or line of text to change it, and move, hide or add sections. Or ask Wave's AI agent in plain words; when Spinnaker has no section for what you want, it writes one in its style. Grids, categories, menus and the blog read your Swell data, so new products appear on their own.

**Free.** Spinnaker costs nothing to install, customize or publish.

**Good to know.** Category tiles look plainer without category images. The cart, search, account and blog pages keep a calmer layout in Spinnaker's colors and type. Spinnaker is a general theme, so it has no size charts, mega menu or sections made for one kind of store, and there is no photo zoom or quick view. Star ratings appear only with a reviews app installed.
